## Topic pillars

- Everyday tech and objects that hide a clever design decision
- Body and brain quirks with a clear scientific explanation
- Money and systems: why prices, queues, rules work the way they do
- History of something ordinary (why the QWERTY layout, why 60 minutes)
- Nature and physics you can notice on your walk home

## Never

- Health, medical or financial advice framed as instructions
- Politics, religion, tragedy, true crime
- Unsourced "studies show" claims
- Clickbait that the payoff does not cash
